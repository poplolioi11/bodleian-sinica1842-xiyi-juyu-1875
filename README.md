# 西醫舉隅 / Xi yi ju yu

《西醫舉隅》（Xi yi ju yu）是德貞（John Dudgeon）由《中西聞見錄》醫學文章摘印的1875年論集，牛津館目著錄印行地為[北京]（Peking），館藏號為Sinica 1842。原書包含五篇序文及心臟、血液運行、眼學與法醫學等篇章，可供晚清醫學史、翻譯史、圖解知識與出版史研究。本版保留已回圖修訂的原題、五序及目錄25面，新增28面完整正文與13個選定圖字／表格層的初錄，並以JSON、TXT及TEI提供文字、註記、疑讀與原件定位；其他母刊既有文字只提供連結，並非全書全文。這是AI輔助整理，未經人類全文校勘，疑讀已標記。

Xi yi ju yu [Gleanings of Western medicine] is an 1875 compilation of John Dudgeon’s medical articles from Zhong xi wen jian lu, catalogued with the place [Peking] and held as Bodleian Sinica 1842. Its five prefaces and articles on the heart, circulation, eyes and forensic medicine support studies of late Qing medicine, translation, visual knowledge and publishing. This release retains 25 source-revised frontmatter faces and adds initial transcriptions of 28 complete prose faces and 13 selected diagram-label or table layers. JSON, TXT and TEI provide the text, notes, uncertainties and source references; existing periodical texts are linked rather than copied. This is a partial-book, AI-assisted edition, without full human collation; uncertain readings are marked.
