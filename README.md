# 西醫舉隅：原題、序文、目錄及來源導覽

版本0.2.0。[倉庫](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1842-xiyi-juyu-1875)與[版本發行頁](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1842-xiyi-juyu-1875/releases/tag/v0.2.0)為本版預定公開位置；製備時尚未取得新版DOI。原0.1.0固定版及其檔案保留。

《西醫舉隅》（Xi yi ju yu）是德貞（John Dudgeon）由《中西聞見錄》醫學文章摘印的1875年論集；牛津館目將印行地著錄為[北京]（Peking），館藏號為Sinica 1842。本冊五篇序文討論醫者的仁心與責任、中西醫書參證及圖解觀察，並記述在京施醫、官員讀者交往與成書刊布的過程，可供晚清醫學史、翻譯史及出版史研究。本版提供原題、五序及跨兩面的目錄共25面AI轉錄與回圖修訂，以JSON、TXT及TEI保存逐面與欄位定位，另附原件和母刊文字入口、疑讀與印章外字記錄；原書正文及圖版沒有在本版重新轉錄。本版結合兩份分開保存的AI讀圖與原圖覆核；疑讀已標記，尚未經人類全文校勘或專家審定。

Xi yi ju yu [Gleanings of Western medicine] is an 1875 compilation of John Dudgeon’s medical articles from Zhong xi wen jian lu, catalogued with the place [Peking] and held as Bodleian Sinica 1842. Its five prefaces discuss medical responsibility, comparison of Chinese and Western medical books, and visual observation, while recounting medical work in Beijing, relationships with officials and readers, and the compilation’s publication. This release supports histories of medicine, translation and publishing through an AI-assisted, source-revised transcription of 25 faces containing the title page, five prefaces and contents. JSON, TXT and TEI preserve face and column references, accompanied by links to the original and existing periodical texts, uncertainty records and seal gaiji. The medical articles and plates are not retranscribed in this release. Two separately saved AI readings were compared and reviewed against the source images. Uncertain readings are marked; the transcription has not undergone full human collation or expert approval.

25面、174個歷史文字／作者印章定位單位，另有1個後來館章記於[COLLECTION-MARKS.json](COLLECTION-MARKS.json)。保留28筆正文疑讀與4個整章印文表示。欄位、原件與分層文字見[book.json](book.json)、[book.txt](book.txt)、[book.tei.xml](book.tei.xml)。四個小字錨點見[READING-MAP.json](READING-MAP.json)，不能把TXT單位串成未分層的連續正文。
