# 西醫舉隅 / Xi yi ju yu

《西醫舉隅》（Xi yi ju yu）是德貞（John Dudgeon）由《中西聞見錄》醫學文章摘印的論集，牛津館目著錄為1875年、[北京]（Peking），館藏號為Sinica 1842。原書包含五篇序文及心臟、血液運行、眼學與法醫學等篇章，可供晚清醫學史、翻譯史、圖解知識與出版史研究。本版保留原題、五序及目錄25面，並提供28面完整正文及13個選定圖字／表格層的修訂文字，以JSON、TXT與TEI保存原字、註記、疑讀及原件定位；其他母刊既有文字只提供連結，並非全書全文。文字經AI獨立讀稿比較及非盲回圖修訂，未經人類全文校勘，未解疑讀已標記。

Xi yi ju yu [Gleanings of Western medicine] compiles John Dudgeon’s medical articles from Zhong xi wen jian lu, catalogued as an 1875 [Peking] edition and held as Bodleian Sinica 1842. Its five prefaces and articles on the heart, circulation, eyes and forensic medicine support studies of late Qing medicine, translation, visual knowledge and publishing. This release preserves 25 title, preface and contents faces and supplies revised text for 28 complete prose faces and 13 selected diagram-label or table layers. JSON, TXT and TEI retain source characters, notes, uncertainties and links to the original; other existing periodical texts are linked rather than copied. AI independent readings were compared through nonblind source-image adjudication; full human collation has not been performed and unresolved readings are marked.

版本 **0.4.0**。英文方括號題名為整理者意譯。[原件](https://digital.bodleian.ox.ac.uk/objects/8203fa5a-7b99-40bf-9642-3ae16068072b/)；[倉庫](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1842-xiyi-juyu-1875)；[本版](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1842-xiyi-juyu-1875/releases/tag/v0.4.0)。本版DOI尚未建立。
