# 西醫舉隅：原題、序文、目錄及來源導覽

Xi yi ju yu [Gleanings of Western medicine]: Prefaces, contents and source guide

版本0.1.0。[倉庫](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1842-xiyi-juyu-1875)與[版本發行頁](https://github.com/poplolioi11/bodleian-sinica1842-xiyi-juyu-1875/releases/tag/v0.1.0)為本版預定公開位置；製備時尚未取得DOI。本版固定原題、五序及目錄的初錄範圍。

《西醫舉隅》（Xi yi ju yu）是德貞（John Dudgeon）由《中西聞見錄》醫學文章摘印的1875年論集；牛津館目將印行地著錄為[北京]（Peking），館藏號為Sinica 1842。本冊五篇序文討論醫者的仁心與責任、中西醫書參證及圖解觀察，並記述在京施醫、官員讀者交往與成書刊布的過程，可供晚清醫學史、翻譯史及出版史研究。本版提供原題、五序及跨兩面的目錄共25面AI初錄，以JSON、TXT及TEI保存逐面與欄位定位，另附原件和母刊文字入口、疑讀與印章外字記錄；原書正文及圖版沒有在本版重新轉錄。疑讀已標記，尚未經人類全文校勘或專家審定。

Xi yi ju yu [Gleanings of Western medicine] is an 1875 compilation of John Dudgeon’s medical articles from Zhong xi wen jian lu, catalogued with the place [Peking] and held as Bodleian Sinica 1842. Its five prefaces discuss medical responsibility, comparison of Chinese and Western medical books, and visual observation, while recounting medical work in Beijing, relationships with officials and readers, and the compilation’s publication. This release supports histories of medicine, translation and publishing through an AI-assisted initial transcription of 25 faces containing the title page, five prefaces and contents. JSON, TXT and TEI preserve face and column references, accompanied by links to the original and existing periodical texts, uncertainty records and seal gaiji. The medical articles and plates are not retranscribed in this release. Uncertain readings are marked; the transcription has not undergone full human collation or expert approval.

共25個書面：原題1面、五序22面、目錄2面，來自13張官方整圖。169個定位單位中保留22筆正文疑讀及4個整章印文外字；外字標記的字串長度不是原刻字數。正文、圖版及附目《五字辯》的未處理層見[PENDING-LAYERS.json](PENDING-LAYERS.json)。
